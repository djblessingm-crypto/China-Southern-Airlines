RESPECT AND TEAMWORK

Our community guidelines.

These are general principles for a positive community experience. Always follow the official rules published by CSN staff on Discord.

Respect everyone

Treat fellow members with courtesy. Harassment, bullying and discrimination do not belong in our community.

Keep things appropriate

Use appropriate language and keep conversations suitable for the community.

Listen to staff

Follow official instructions and use the correct channels when you need assistance.

Protect your information

Never share passwords, private account details or sensitive personal information.
